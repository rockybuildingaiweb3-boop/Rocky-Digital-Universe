# 1. Purpose

World Architecture defines how **RockyOS** is organized, explored, and experienced.

Unlike a traditional website consisting of isolated, transactional pages, RockyOS is architected as an **interconnected digital universe**. 

Visitors should never feel that they are clicking through disconnected web pages or scanning a lifeless digital brochure. Instead, they should feel that they are traversing an organic, living digital ecosystem where every section breathes, every interaction has intention, and every discovery reveals a deeper layer of Rocky's journey.

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

RockyOS is not a collection of pages. It is an **ecosystem of living dimensions**.

- **Everything is Connected**: No project exists without the mental model that birthed it; no skill exists without the timeline that forged it; no code exists without the human purpose that drives it.
- **Reward Curiosity**: Navigation should not merely provide access — it should invite wonder. Exploration is treated as a core feature.
- **Narrative Continuity**: The visitor's journey flows like an interactive film: from the Human-AI opening prologue, into the galactic Universe Map, through specialized world nodes, and back into the central orbit.
- **Living Growth**: The architecture is built with an infinite expansion horizon (2024–2034) so that a decade of compounding experience can be housed without structural redesign.

## World 01 — Identity (Who I Am)

> **Theme**: *Human · Story · Memory · Purpose*  
> *「剥离技术标签，回归真实的生命底色。」*

### Core Mission
To allow collaborators, employers, and friends to deeply understand the person behind the code: the origins, values, philosophy, and taste that govern Rocky's decisions.

### Structural Dimensions & Sub-Pages:
1. **The Origin Story (个人故事)**: Narrative biography tracing the journey from early curiosity to professional builder.
2. **Core Values (核心价值观)**: Principles of craftsmanship, intellectual integrity, extreme ownership, and long-termism.
3. **Formative Timeline (人生时间线)**: Major life chapters, crossroads, transformations, and milestones.
4. **Curated Gallery & Travel (生活影像与足迹)**: Visual snapshots of the physical world explored, cities visited, and offline memories.
5. **Books & Mental Influences (思想源泉)**: The thinkers, philosophers, and books that shaped Rocky's worldview.
6. **Life Philosophy & 10-Year Horizon (未来愿景)**: Personal compass and aspirations for the decade ahead.

# 6. Global Navigation & Omnipresent Controls

Navigation must never break immersion. The visitor must always subconsciously know:
1. **Where they are**
2. **Where they came from**
3. **Where they can go next**

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [ROCKYOS]  [Identity · Capability · Knowledge · Lab · Connect · Growth]  [⌘K] [EN|ZH] |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 6.1 Minimal Floating OS Topbar
- **Sticky Glass Header**: `72px` height, frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`), ultra-thin border.
- **Active World Indicator**: Subtle cyan under-glow highlighting the active world.
- **Telemetry Beacon**: Live blinking green light (`ONLINE`) and real-time UTC/local clock.
- **Instant Language Switcher**: High-contrast pill toggle `[ EN | 简体中文 ]` with zero-refresh translation.

### 6.2 The Command Palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
Inspired by modern developer interfaces (VS Code, Raycast, Linear):
- **Trigger**: Pressing `⌘K` (Mac) or `Ctrl+K` (Windows/Linux) or clicking the search icon opens a centered spotlight palette.
- **Capabilities**:
  - **Quick Jump**: Type `cap` -> Jump to Capability World; `lab` -> Jump to Laboratory.
  - **Search Everything**: Instant fuzzy search across all projects, articles, tools, and growth logs.
  - **Execute Actions**: Switch language (`Switch to Chinese`), toggle theme (`Toggle Light Mode`), copy WeChat ID.
  - **Keyboard Friendly**: Full arrow-key navigation, `Enter` to select, `Esc` to dismiss.

### 6.3 Contextual Breadcrumbs
Every sub-page displays a clean breadcrumb trail with glowing separators:
```
Universe > Capability World > Projects > AI Automation Pipeline
```

# 7. Unified Search & Knowledge Retrieval

Search is a first-class feature across the universe:
- **Global Index**: Client-side in-memory search index covering:
  - Project names, tech stacks, and descriptions.
  - Articles, mental models, and tags.
  - Growth logs, dates, and milestone keywords.
  - Commands and quick action triggers.
- **Zero Latency**: Instant typing results (< 10ms) powered by lightweight token matching.
- **Future AI Search Hook**: Dedicated architectural reserve for RockyGPT vector search in future phases.

# 11. Core Architectural Principles

- **Principle 01: Everything is Connected** (Projects connect to Knowledge; Knowledge connects to Experiments; Experiments connect to Growth).
- **Principle 02: Everything Tells a Story** (No isolated widgets or meaningless decorative fluff).
- **Principle 03: Every Interaction Has Intention** (Hover, motion, and feedback communicate physical reality).
- **Principle 04: Every Page Creates Value** (If an element teaches nothing, inspires nothing, or helps nobody, eliminate it).
- **Principle 05: Exploration Over Navigation** (Delight the user when they wander off the beaten path).
- **Principle 06: Growth Never Ends** (A digital universe that compounds alongside life).
